        Data and estimates

        Google Maps searches multiple areas across Boston. The planner checks your full trip time for those results and shows up to ten that fit, sorted by rating and then review count. Trip costs use Google’s starting meal price, a 20% tax and tip estimate, and fares when available. The transit option can include buses, subway, light rail, commuter rail, and other scheduled service in Google Maps. The planner assumes a 25-minute meal and uses an approximate departure time for return transit. Check current service, prices, and hours before leaving.
